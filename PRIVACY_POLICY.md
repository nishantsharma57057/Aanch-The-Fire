## 2. Information we may collect
Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:
- Contact and booking details, such as your name, phone number, email address (if provided), preferred date and time, guest count and occasion.
- Order details, such as dishes, quantities, selected options, delivery or pickup preference, delivery address or location details where needed, and instructions you provide.
- Payment references and transaction status needed to confirm an online payment. Card, UPI and other payment credentials are handled by the payment provider; we do not ask you to send payment passwords, PINs or OTPs to the restaurant.
- Messages, feedback, allergy or dietary information that you choose to share with us.
- Basic technical information made available by your browser or hosting/security services, such as device, browser, IP address and website usage logs.

Please share only information needed to handle your request. If you provide allergy or dietary information, please also speak directly with our team before ordering; we cannot guarantee an allergen-free kitchen or preparation environment.

## 3. How we use information
We use relevant information to:
- Receive, prepare, fulfil and manage food orders and payment confirmations.
- Handle table booking requests, pre-orders, celebrations and customer enquiries.
- Contact you about an order, booking, availability, delivery, pickup, changes or support.
- Maintain transaction, accounting, security and operational records.
- Improve website performance, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Payments and service providers
Online payments are processed through Razorpay or the payment method shown at checkout. Payment providers may collect and process information under their own privacy notices and terms. We receive transaction details such as payment status and payment references so that we can reconcile and fulfil your order. Please review the relevant provider’s policy when using its checkout.

We may also rely on website hosting, communications, analytics, security and other service providers to operate the website and restaurant services. They may process information on our behalf or under their own terms, as applicable.

## 5. When information may be shared
We share information only as reasonably needed:
- With payment providers to process and verify transactions.
- With delivery or other fulfilment partners, where used, to complete your order.
- With hosting, IT, communications and security providers supporting our operations.
- With authorities or other parties when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to address fraud and security issues.

## 6. Retention and security
We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage orders, bookings, customer support, accounting, legal obligations and disputes. Retention periods may vary by record type and applicable law.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not share OTPs, passwords, card PINs or banking credentials with anyone claiming to represent the restaurant.
